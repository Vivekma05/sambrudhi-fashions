Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information Samruddhi Fashion ("we", "us") handles when you use our website, vivekma05.github.io/sambrudhi-fashions, and our Android app. The app shows the same store as the website, so this policy covers both.

Information you give us when you order

To place an order you enter your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, pincode and, if you wish, a note. Together with the items in your bag, these details are written into a WhatsApp message on your own device. They reach us only when you press send in WhatsApp.

We use this information only to:

  • confirm your order, its availability and the total amount;
  • arrange delivery, including sharing your name, phone number and address with the courier that delivers your parcel;
  • contact you about your order, payment, delivery, exchanges or questions you ask us.

Information kept on your device

Your bag, your wishlist and the delivery details you last used (so you don't have to type them again) are saved in the storage of your browser or the app on your device. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser, or the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Payments

We do not collect card or bank details. If you pay by UPI, the payment is made in your own UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm) and is handled by that app and your bank under their own terms. Cash on delivery is paid to the delivery person.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or a password.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Services we rely on

These providers handle limited technical information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts our website and keeps standard server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on the pages. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp carries the order messages and our chats with you. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

How long we keep your information

Order messages stay in our WhatsApp chat as our record of your order, for as long as we need them for delivery, exchanges, accounts and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your messages and details at any time, unless we are required by law to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Children

Our store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
